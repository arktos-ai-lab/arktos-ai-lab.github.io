Talitha.

Talitha puts research papers into more languages. The collection now holds the RAIL workshop papers from 2020 to 2024 (64 papers), translated into French and Swahili next to the English original, at talithapapers.com.

English Français Kiswahili

How it works

01 · Open models

Translated by open models.

Each paper is translated by openly available models, not by a commercial service.

02 · Automated check

Checked against the original.

An automated review compares each translation with the original paper. A human has not reviewed the translations. Cite the original paper, not the translation.

03 · Licence

One licence per paper.

Every paper keeps its own licence, shown on its page together with a link to the source.
