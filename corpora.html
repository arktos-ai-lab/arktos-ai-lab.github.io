The Text Corpora Index.

A public, citable index of the text corpus families reviewed for Open but Unvetted (Proceedings of RAIL 2026 at LREC 2026). This index covers written-language resources only. Speech, audio, image, and video datasets are out of scope here, even where a listed project also ships those modalities. Each row is a license-tier assignment, verified against the primary license page rather than the dataset card. Intended as a working artifact for NLP practitioners building text datasets in African-language contexts.

Subset

This index is a subset of the tier assignments in the published paper. The full machine-readable release, with citations and the compatibility matrix, is in preparation. Where this page and the paper differ, the paper is authoritative. Corrections and additions are welcome via LinkedIn.

Tier Legend

T1
Public domain / CC0
No rights reserved. Freely combinable.
T2
Permissive (CC-BY, MIT, Apache)
Attribution-only. Commercial and derivative use allowed.
T3
Share-alike (CC-BY-SA)
Propagates. Derivatives must be released under the same license.
T4a
NonCommercial (NC)
Research only. Incompatible with share-alike chains.
T4b
No-Derivs (ND)
No derivative works permitted. Blocks annotation and fine-tuning datasets.
T5
Prohibited / ToS violation
Not permissible to redistribute. Retain only as reference.
