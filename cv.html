Profile

Independent researcher working at the intersection of machine learning data provenance, low-resource language NLP, and the law and economics of AI. Founder of Arktos AI Lab, an independent research lab in the Netherlands that works with Claude, Anthropic's AI, as a research partner. Publishing at NLP, digital humanities and legal informatics venues and in international tax and economics journals.

Russia's crisis responses through the macroeconomic trilemma, 1998-2024: from financial shocks to constraint shocks.Journal article

With Busola Ijaware. Russian Journal of Economics 12(3), 358-389. doi:10.32609/j.ruje.12.190983

Distinguishes financial shocks, which occur within functioning market structures, from constraint shocks, which impair the infrastructure of exchange, and classifies policy trade-offs in four Russian crisis episodes with the macroeconomic trilemma.

May 2026

Open but Unvetted: the ethics of African language data.Workshop paper

Proceedings of RAIL 2026 at LREC 2026, pp. 128-139.

An empirical audit of Creative Commons license provenance across more than twenty corpus families used in African NLP, with three case-study languages (Kituba/Munukutuba, Zarma and Moore). Documents four failure modes from primary sources (JW300, WAXAL, Tanzil and the Congolese Radio Corpus) and proposes a due-diligence checklist.

Nov 2025

Beyond the Single Market: the case for clustering cross-border comparables in pan-European comparability studies.Journal article

International Transfer Pricing Journal 32(6), IBFD. doi:10.59403/ka7jpq

A five-phase method for pan-European comparability analyses: database screening, quantitative filtering, manual review, comparability adjustments and interquartile ranges, with market clustering by geography, GDP, labour costs and sovereign risk.
